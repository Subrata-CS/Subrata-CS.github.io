Subrata Pramanik

IIIT Allahabad. Visual Document Understanding | Multimodal Retrieval & Reasoning

I am a Research Scholar in Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition at IIIT Allahabad, working in the iMeDIA Laboratory under Dr. Mohammed Javed. My research focuses on visual document understanding and retrieval-augmented generation with multimodal large language models.

A large part of my recent work is measurement-first: isolating which component of a complex pipeline actually produces a reported gain. In my current study on multi-agent document understanding, controlled ensemble ablations on DocVQA and InfographicVQA show that improvements widely credited to trained routers and orchestration are largely explained by model heterogeneity alone.

I also work on visual document retrieval, studying how embedding compression degrades fine-grained local queries far more than page-level global ones, and on query-adaptive re-encoding as a way to keep retrieval quality without paying full multi-vector storage cost. Alongside this I am completing a channel-centric survey of retrieval-augmented visual document understanding with collaborators including Prof. David Doermann (University at Buffalo).

Beyond research, I build things. I created Open, a free computer-science learning platform with 578 topics and an in-browser code runner for 18 languages. I also run Subrata Sikha Niketan, an education platform from Prayagraj that prepares students across India for competitive examinations.

Whether you are a student starting out in computer vision, or a researcher working on document AI, feel free to connect with me through the links below.

Diversity, Not Orchestration: Rethinking What Makes Multi-Agent Document Understanding Work
[In preparation for CVPR 2027] With Javed M., Doermann D.
Paper / Code (coming soon)

A measurement study of multi-agent document VQA. Controlled ensemble composition ablations on DocVQA and InfographicVQA (n = 2000 each, ANLS) show that heterogeneous-model voting outperforms same-model self-consistency (+0.008 on DocVQA, p = 0.006; +0.041 on InfographicVQA, p < 0.0001), while diversity alone is insufficient without member quality.

Visual Document Understanding with Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Channel-Centric Survey
[Under review at IEEE TPAMI] With Javed M., Doermann D.
Paper / Supplementary (coming soon)

A channel-centric taxonomy of how visual, textual and layout information flows through retrieval-augmented document pipelines. Built from a systematic search of 60 venues, covering 356 references and 47 datasets, with a companion meta-analysis of reported results.

Query-Adaptive Re-encoding for Compression-Robust Visual Document Retrieval
[Submitted to IEEE RAIT 2026] With Javed M.
Paper / Code (coming soon)

Embedding compression degrades local region-level queries roughly 3–4× more than global page-level queries. We propose query-adaptive re-encoding that recovers accuracy without storing full-precision multi-vector embeddings, evaluated with ColQwen2-2B and Qwen2-VL-2B-Instruct across five ViDoRe subsets (2347 pages) on a manually labelled query set (Cohen's κ ≥ 0.70).
