Hi, I am Subrata Pramanik, an M.Tech + Ph.D. researcher in the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad, where I work in the iMeDIA Laboratory advised by Dr. Mohammed Javed, with external supervision from Prof. David Doermann at the University at Buffalo.

My research is on visual document understanding with retrieval-augmented generation — teaching multimodal models to read a document the way a person does: finding the right page or region first, then reasoning over what is actually there. I am interested in how visual, textual and layout information should flow through a retrieval pipeline, and in careful measurement: which part of a complex system is genuinely responsible for a result, and which part only appears to be.

Alongside research I am a teaching assistant at IIIT Allahabad, I built Open, a free computer-science learning platform, and I founded Subrata Sikha Niketan, an education platform for students preparing for competitive examinations.
