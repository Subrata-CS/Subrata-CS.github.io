About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad, doing an integrated M.Tech + Ph.D. in Information Technology. I work in the iMeDIA Lab with Dr. Mohammed Javed, and Prof. David Doermann (University at Buffalo) is my external supervisor.

My research is on visual document understanding. A lot of the documents we use every day, like scanned forms, reports, tables, charts and infographics, carry meaning in their layout and not just in their text. I am interested in how vision-language models read pages like these, how to retrieve the right page or region before the model answers (multimodal RAG), and how to evaluate these systems fairly on benchmarks such as DocVQA, InfographicVQA and ChartQA.

Before starting my Ph.D., I worked at Tata Consultancy Services from August 2022 to March 2024, mostly writing Python and SQL to automate data checks for a global technology client. At the institute I am also a teaching assistant for Problem Solving with Programming and Data Analytics.

I am looking for research internships for Summer 2027. If you work on document AI or multimodal retrieval, I would be happy to hear from you.
