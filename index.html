Subrata Pramanik

IIIT Allahabad | Visual Document Understanding with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

I am an M.Tech + Ph.D. researcher at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad, working in the iMeDIA Laboratory under Dr. Mohammed Javed, with external supervision from Prof. David Doermann (University at Buffalo).

My research is on visual document understanding with retrieval-augmented generation — teaching multimodal models to read a document the way a person does: finding the right page or region first, then reasoning over what is actually there. I am interested in how visual, textual and layout information should flow through a retrieval pipeline, and in careful measurement: which part of a complex system is genuinely responsible for a result, and which part only appears to be.

Outside research, I built Open, a free computer-science learning platform. I enjoy making difficult material approachable, which is also what I do as a teaching assistant.

If you are a student getting started in computer vision, or a researcher working on document AI, feel free to get in touch.
